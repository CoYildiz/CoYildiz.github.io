## Why The Writing Blog Was Hard for Me

I've started writing a blog because I want to share my thoughts about films, books, and other things I consume. However, apart from sharing my programming notes, I haven't been able to write even a full page about these topics.

At first, I chose Ghost in the Shell as my topic. Since it was both my first attempt at writing a non-technical blog post for the first time and I wanted to improve my English, I thought it would be a good place to start. Unfortunately, I wasn't satisfied with the result.

I assumed that choosing a topic and writing down my thoughts would be easy. In reality, it turned out to be much harder than I expected. Because of that, I decided to write about the difficulty itself.

## Choosing a Topic

Choosing a topic is not actually the hard part. The difficult part is figuring out what you want to say about that topic and how to say that.

I picked several topics and wrote a few paragraphs about them. However, I often found myself getting lost. Instead of developing my ideas, I kept changing the subject. It became a cycle: pick a topic, write something, dislike it, and then pick another topic.

## English Knowledge

I started this blog to improve my English, but sometimes it feels as if I'm only writing fragments of ideas rather than complete thoughts. Maybe that's part of the process. Learning to write is not only about learning new words; it's also about learning how to explore an idea and stay with it long enough to understand what you really think.  I probably spend more time rewriting sentences than actually writing. I use LLMs to help me structure my thoughts, but I try to keep the ideas and opinions entirely my own. But I still feel like it's not enough.

## What am I Going to do?

Actually, I decided to search some blogs and looking for some English sentence patterns. 

When I was in the prep class, I was writing a lot of essays for homework and classes, and I was actually good at it at the time. At least, I feel like I did but writing a blog feels like a failure in my writing skills, so probably I am going to do search and study a little bit.

So I decided that this would be the first post of my non-technical blog, because I realized that my struggle with writing is actually part of the process I want to document here. This blog is not only about sharing ideas, but also about learning how to form them.
